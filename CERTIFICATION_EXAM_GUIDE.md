# NeuroTrace Certification Exam Guide

## Overview

The **NeuroTrace Certification Exam** is a comprehensive, timed assessment designed to prepare EEG technologists for professional certification exams. This is an original NeuroTrace Academy feature that provides realistic exam conditions and detailed performance analysis.

## Exam Format

### Specifications
- **Duration:** 120 minutes (2 hours)
- **Questions:** 100 multiple-choice questions
- **Passing Score:** 70% (recommended benchmark)
- **Format:** Computer-based with real-time timer
- **Navigation:** Full question navigator with flagging system

### Domain Distribution

The exam mirrors professional certification distributions:

| Domain | Coverage | Questions |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Domain I: Pre-Study Procedures** | ~25% | ~25 questions |
| **Domain II: Performing the EEG Study** | ~35% | ~35 questions |
| **Domain III: Post-Study Procedures** | ~25% | ~25 questions |
| **Domain IV: Ethics & Professional Issues** | ~15% | ~15 questions |

## How to Take the Exam

### Before You Start

**Preparation:**
1. Study all four domains thoroughly
2. Review EEG patterns and procedures
3. Practice with domain-specific quizzes
4. Ensure you have 2+ hours of uninterrupted time

**Technical Requirements:**
- Stable internet connection
- Desktop or laptop (mobile not recommended)
- Modern web browser
- Quiet testing environment

### During the Exam

**Step 1: Introduction Screen**
- Review exam format and instructions
- Check domain distribution
- Understand the time limit
- Click "Start Certification Exam" when ready

**Step 2: Taking the Exam**
- Timer starts immediately
- Read each question carefully
- Select your answer by clicking an option
- Use "Flag" button to mark questions for review
- Navigate using Previous/Next or question navigator
- Monitor time remaining in header

**Step 3: Review & Submit**
- Check question navigator for unanswered questions
- Review flagged questions
- Click "Submit Exam" when complete
- Confirm submission (cannot undo)

### After Submission

**Results Page:**
- Overall score displayed immediately
- Domain-by-domain breakdown
- Performance analysis
- Personalized recommendations
- Option to retake exam

## Strategies for Success

### Time Management

**Pacing:**
- 100 questions in 120 minutes = ~1.2 minutes per question
- Don't spend too long on any single question
- Flag difficult questions and move on
- Reserve time for review

**Time Allocation:**
- First pass: 80-90 minutes (answer all questions)
- Review flagged: 20-30 minutes
- Final check: 10 minutes

### Answering Questions

**Best Practices:**
1. Read the question completely before looking at options
2. Eliminate obviously incorrect answers first
3. Look for key words and qualifiers
4. Apply critical thinking to scenarios
5. Trust your training and instincts

**Flagging Strategy:**
- Flag if you're uncertain between two options
- Flag if you need to think longer
- Flag if you want to verify during review
- Don't flag excessively (defeats the purpose)

### Domain-Specific Tips

**Domain I: Pre-Study Procedures**
- Know electrode placement (10-20 system)
- Understand patient preparation
- Review safety protocols
- Study infection control procedures

**Domain II: Performing the EEG Study**
- Master montage construction
- Understand artifact identification
- Know technical adjustments
- Review activation procedures

**Domain III: Post-Study Procedures**
- Study documentation requirements
- Know report components
- Understand quality assurance
- Review data management

**Domain IV: Ethics & Professional**
- Understand patient rights
- Know professional standards
- Review ethical guidelines
- Study legal requirements

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many times can I take the exam?**
A: Unlimited. Retake as often as needed for practice.

**Q: Does this grant professional certification?**
A: No, this is a preparation tool for professional exams.

**Q: Are questions the same each time?**
A: No, questions are randomized from a large pool.

**Q: What if I run out of time?**
A: Exam auto-submits with your current answers.

**Q: Can I pause the exam?**
A: No, timer runs continuously once started.

**Q: Is 70% good enough?**
A: 70% is passing, but aim for 85%+ for confidence.

**Q: How long should I study before taking?**
