# Home Page Messaging Update - EEG Technologist Focus

## Overview
Updated the home page messaging to accurately reflect that NeuroTrace Academy is focused on **EEG Technologist training** rather than clinical interpretation. This better aligns with the R. EEG T. (Registered EEG Technologist) ABRET certification focus.

## Changes Made

### Hero Section
**Before:**
- "Master EEG Interpretation with AI-Powered Learning"
- Focus on interpretation and clinical analysis

**After:**
- "Become an Expert EEG Technologist"
- "Master EEG recording techniques, pattern recognition, and technical skills. Prepare for ABRET R. EEG T. certification with comprehensive training modules."

### Feature Cards
1. **Clinical Cases** → **Training Cases**
   - Changed from "expert analysis" to "technical challenges and learning points"
   - Subtitle: "Technical Practice" (was "Real-world Learning")

2. **Pattern Library**
   - From: "Comprehensive atlas of normal and abnormal EEG patterns"
   - To: "Learn to recognize and identify EEG patterns as a skilled technologist"
   - Subtitle: "Recognize & Document" (was "Learn & Identify")

3. **Technical Workflow** (was "Clinical Workflow")
   - From: "Best practices for recording and interpretation"
   - To: "Master recording techniques, electrode application, and quality control"

4. **Syndromes**
   - From: "Epilepsy syndromes with EEG correlations and diagnostic criteria"
   - To: "Understand epilepsy syndromes and their characteristic EEG patterns"
   - Subtitle: "Know the Patterns" (was "Master Classifications")

5. **Practice Quizzes**
   - Updated to "ABRET R. EEG T. exam-style questions" (was "ABRET-style questions")
   - Subtitle: "R. EEG T. Prep" (was "ABRET Prep")
